## New features and improvements

- Qwen3.8-Flash-Next asks the kernel for the n-gram rows of the next verify window while the draft chain still runs. The window's first token is known when the chain starts and each draft is known at the host read between chain steps, so their rows of the NVMe-backed table are queued (`MADV_WILLNEED`, nothing is waited for) while the GPU runs the next step, and the gather at the window finds the pages resident instead of faulting them. The gathered bytes are the same with or without it (`REXL3_PLE_PREFETCH=0` turns it off). Measured on one GB10 (decode screen of six prompts plus the concurrency sweep, replies exact, two runs each, same node): 75.2 to 75.9 tok/s drafted decode, 92.1 to 94.6 tok/s aggregate on two streams and 109.5 to 111.5 on four.
