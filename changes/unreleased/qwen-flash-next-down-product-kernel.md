## New features and improvements

- Qwen3.8-Flash-Next prompt prefill computes the hyper-connection pre-mix's down product (the 320-column product over the four normed streams) in the engine's own kernel instead of two cuBLASLt split-K calls, with every output bit unchanged (the mixed rows, the inject rows and the post-injected streams are tested equal to the 64-row cuBLAS slab loop on the 4,096-row chunk grid and at ragged row counts, on data spread over six orders of magnitude). The library's accumulation order was reproduced from the outside with crafted inputs and then checked on random data: 64-column stages cut across four splits, three interleaved MMA chains a split added in an order that depends on the output's 16-column group, the four splits added in order, the lo plane's total plus the hi plane's. The kernel forms the normed rows on the fly from the post-inject streams, so the two 84 MB fp16 planes per pre-mix are no longer written by the norm pass and read back by the product. Measured on one GB10 at the 2,200 MHz clock cap, one pre-mix of 4,096 rows with the inject product and the fused post-inject takes 5.20 ms where it took 6.11 to 6.27 ms.
