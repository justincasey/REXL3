## New features and improvements

- The MTP draft chain's token staging, embedding lookup, head-input normalisation, stream combine and token pick launch as programmatic dependent launches, and the head-input kernel reads its weights before the wait and its input row once. Every output bit is unchanged (tests with the launch attribute off and on, 24 repetitions of four dependent steps, equal to the recorded bits). Measured on one GB10 (nsys of the decode screen, six prompts, same boot): the kernels this commit and the pick change touch cost 63.3 us per draft step before and 21.3 us after (the head input 11.4 to 3.7 us, the stage 1.8 to 1.0 us, the combine 2.2 to 1.7 us). Decode screen on one node, base/candidate/candidate/base: 71.66, 72.23, 72.74, 72.05 tok/s drafted and 38.01, 38.54, 38.61, 38.11 tok/s serial, `tokens_per_round` 2.9194 throughout (the two commits together).
