## New features and improvements

- Prompt prefill of the Qwen3.8-Flash-Next gated delta net layers applies the head's gated RMS norm inside the recurrence kernel instead of in a pass of its own, with every output bit and every recurrent-state bit unchanged (the normed output, the state of every slot and the conv history are tested equal to the recurrence followed by the norm kernel at lengths on and across the 16-step tile, odd totals, several sequences per launch and chunks of 4,012 and 4,096 rows). The recurrence kernel now keeps all 128 value rows of a head in one block, ends each tile by normalising its steps in the norm kernel's own order (sum of squares over the same lanes and butterfly, same rounding of the sigmoid of z) and writes the result once, so the 49 MB it used to write and read back a layer is gone. One layer of a 4,012-row chunk takes 2.44 ms for the recurrence plus norm instead of 2.06 + 0.59 ms.
