## New features and improvements

- Qwen3.8-Flash-Next prompt prefill runs the hyper-connection pre-mix's up product and mix on 64-row by 64-column tiles of all four streams (16 warps a CTA) instead of 32 by 32. The products read half as many operand bytes from the L2 (the up weights and the low rows are shared by four times the outputs) and the epilogue loads and stores whole 32-byte sectors (x and the mixed rows were read and written in 16-byte halves of a sector). Each output keeps its own accumulation chains and the streams are still summed in stream order, so every output bit is unchanged (the mixed rows, the inject rows and the post-injected streams stay equal to the 64-row slab loop at ragged row counts). On a 4,016-row chunk the kernel takes 1.22 ms instead of 1.58 ms.
