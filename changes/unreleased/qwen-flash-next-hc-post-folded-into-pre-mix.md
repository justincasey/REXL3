## New features and improvements

- In decode and verify windows the previous block's hyper-connection post-inject no longer runs as a launch of its own: the two kernels of the next pre-mix read the updated streams (the same fused multiply-add per element, so the streams, the mixed rows and the inject coefficients keep every bit) and the second one writes them back. A test pins the folded call to the post-inject launch followed by the pre-mix at one to eight rows and at row counts that run several passes, with the programmatic launch attribute off and on. Measured on one GB10 (decode screen, six prompts, base/candidate/base/candidate on the same node and boot count): 76.24 and 76.26 tok/s drafted before, 76.64 and 76.40 after; serial decode 38.18 and 38.15 before, 38.39 and 38.32 after.
