## New features and improvements

- Qwen3.8-Flash-Next prompt prefill runs the hyper-connection pre-mix's norm pass and its inject product (the 4-column product over the four normed streams) in one kernel, with every output bit unchanged (the mixed rows, the inject rows and the post-injected streams are tested equal to the 64-row slab loop at ragged row counts, with and without the fused post-inject). The library's inject launches read the whole fp16 hi/lo split of the normed rows a second time; the kernel instead runs the library's 20 split-K chains itself (one chain of m16n8k16 MMAs per 512 columns, added in split order, the lo sum plus the hi sum) on the tiles it has just formed in shared memory, 16 rows of one stream a CTA with two CTAs an SM so that a stream slab is read back from L2 between its two passes. On a steady 4,096-row chunk the norm, inject and split-reduce launches take 308 ms before and 288 ms after (nsys, one GB10).
