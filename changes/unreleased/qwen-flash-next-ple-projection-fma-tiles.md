## New features and improvements

- Prompt prefill of Qwen3.8-Flash-Next computes the two per-layer-embedding projections of a chunk (2,560 to 10,240 and 2,560 to 2,560 columns) in 128 x 128 tiles with one fused multiply-add a term, with every output bit unchanged (tested equal to the previous kernel on one to 4,012 rows, tail tiles, widths that are not multiples of the tiles, an inner size that is not a multiple of 8, and values spanning the half-precision range). A half-precision product is exact in 32-bit floating point, so adding it with one fused operation gives the same number as rounding the product and then adding, in the same ascending order over the inner dimension; the previous kernel spent two instructions on every term. On a 4,012-row chunk the two projections take 18.4 ms instead of 34.3 ms (15.1 and 3.2 ms against 27.7 and 6.6).
