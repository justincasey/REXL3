## New features and improvements

- Qwen3.8-Flash-Next prompt prefill runs the gated delta-net layers' conv, norm and gate kernel beside the recurrence of the rows before it. A chunk of at least 2,048 rows is cut into up to four pieces of rows; the conv kernel of piece i + 1 runs on a second stream in a form that needs 48 registers a thread, 16 KB of shared memory and four warps an SM (a warp walks 128 rows of one 128-channel unit with its conv weights in shared memory, the previous three rows in registers and the next rows in a cp.async ring), so a block of it fits beside the recurrence kernel, which holds 59 K of an SM's 64 K registers; the recurrence over piece i waits only for the conv piece that wrote its rows, and the state pool carries it from piece to piece as it does from chunk to chunk. Every output bit is unchanged (the conv piece equals the previous kernel's workspace byte for byte at every block count and row cut, the pieces equal one launch bit for bit three times over, and a real-pack prompt gives the same last-row logits with and without the pieces at chunk widths of 8,192, 2,500 and 1,000 rows). Measured on one GB10 with nsys on a steady 8,108-row chunk of a 32,748-token prompt: a layer's conv, recurrence and commit take 4.73 ms against 5.85 ms in order, and the chunk's span goes from 3,984.9 to 3,941.2 ms (the recurrence pieces cost 4.4 ms a layer against 4.2 ms whole). In the harness, same session, 32K-token cold prefill on one GB10 ran 2,041.2 and 2,041.6 tok/s with the pieces against 2,029.8 and 2,023.7 tok/s without (+0.7 %, inside the run-to-run noise but in the same direction in both pairs), with all nine pinned replies exact. `REXL3_PF_PIPELINE=0` restores the in-order launches.
