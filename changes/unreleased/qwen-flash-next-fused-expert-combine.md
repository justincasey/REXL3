## New features and improvements

- Qwen3.8-Flash-Next prompt prefill folds the shared expert's finish into the routed experts' combine, with every output bit unchanged (the fused launch is tested equal to the two launches it replaces, on gates whose sigmoid saturates and on unrouted slots). The routed sum used to be written to an fp32 buffer and read back by the finish launch; it now stays in registers and the one bf16 rounding happens in the combine, which also frees that 4,096-row fp32 buffer (42 MB). Measured on one GB10 with a 4,096-row layer: combine plus finish 2.70 to 2.14 ms. On a real cold 32K-token prompt a steady chunk's combine and finish launches take 120 to 102 ms, and with the one-launch expert GEMMs the cold 32K prefill runs at 1,504 tok/s against 1,473 before.
