## New features and improvements

- Qwen3.8-Flash-Next's tiled prefill runs its dense linears (the linear-attention and full-attention projections, 6- to 8-bit EXL3) through a 128 x 128 tile that two CTAs share an SM with, instead of the 64 x 256 tile that took the whole SM's shared memory for one CTA. One CTA's epilogue and pipeline fill now hide under the other's tensor-core work. Each output's K sum is still one chain of m16n8k16 MMAs over the k tiles in order from +0.0 with the same decoder and epilogue, so every output bit is unchanged (a test compares the new kernel with the old one bit for bit at 1 to 4,096 rows, with a bias, for three output types, on odd strip counts and on adversarial rows). Measured on one GB10 at 2,200 MHz, 4,096 rows per call, weights from DRAM: in_proj_qkv 3.65 to 2.83 ms, in_proj_z 2.21 to 1.76 ms, out_proj 2.52 to 2.10 ms, q_proj 4.21 to 3.31 ms; the dense linears of one chunk add up to 510 ms before and 411 ms after (rotation included).
