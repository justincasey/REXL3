## New features and improvements

- In Qwen3.8-Flash-Next decode and verify windows of one to eight rows, the 4-warp EXL3 projections with a wide grid (the gated-delta-net `in_proj_z` and `out_proj` and the attention `o_proj`: 84 of a window's 169 linears) rotate their own input inside the linear kernel instead of waiting for a `rot_in` launch ahead of it. Each warp rotates, in shared memory, the 128-column blocks of the input it multiplies with the statements of the launch it replaces, so every output bit is unchanged (a test pins the fused call to the launch pair at every width and codebook, fp16, bf16 and fp32 activations, 1 to 40 rows, the programmatic launch attribute on and off, and the K-split and strip cuts). The 8-warp projections and the narrow ones (`in_proj_qkv`, `q_proj`, `k_proj`, `v_proj`, the indexer) keep the launch: measured, the rotation's latency inside their kernels costs more than the launch saved. `REXL3_EXL3_FUSE=0` turns the fusion off. Measured on one GB10 (nsys of the six-prompt decode screen, one binary, `REXL3_EXL3_FUSE=0` against 1): a window's `rot_in` launches fell from 169 to 85 and their exclusive time from 457 to 315 us with the linears' time unchanged (11.1 ms), and the window spans at three, four and eight rows fell from 31,466 to 31,144 us, 34,541 to 34,281 us and 47,439 to 47,264 us. The engine's own verify time per window, read without the profiler, fell by 0.15 ms on average; the decode screen (78.00 tok/s with and without, four runs each) does not resolve a change that small.
