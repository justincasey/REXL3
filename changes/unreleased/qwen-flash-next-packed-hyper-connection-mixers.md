## New features and improvements

- The decode and verify windows of up to three rows read the hyper-connection mixers (down, inject and up weights, 13.3 MB a call) from a lossless 13-bit packed copy made at load: the weights are bf16 numbers the loader rounded to fp16, so a raw byte and a five-bit code hold each one exactly, and the decoded floats are the fp16 weights widened (every sum is unchanged). Each packed tensor is decoded back and compared with its fp16 weight value by value before it is used; a tensor outside the format keeps the fp16 path, and so do windows of four or more rows, where the packed kernels measured no faster. The copy adds 1.0 GiB to the weight plan (`REXL3_HCX_PACK=0` turns it off) and takes 0.13 s to make. Measured on one GB10: a captured graph of 24 mixers at three rows takes 57.8 us a call from fp16 and 48.2 us from the packed copy (one row 57.5 and 49.2). Decode window spans in an nsys profile of the six decode prompts, fp16 then packed: one row 27.06 and 25.96 ms, two rows 29.12 and 28.36 ms, three rows 32.22 and 31.17 ms. Full harness runs on two nodes, previous build then this one (all nine replies equal to the reference, drafted equal to serial): decode 76.30 and 76.52 against 77.40 and 77.60 tok/s, serial decode 38.38 and 38.54 against 40.01 and 40.21, prefill at 32K 1,471 and 1,479 against 1,470 and 1,480 tok/s; memory available at the end of the run 13.6 against 12.5 and 12.8 GiB.
