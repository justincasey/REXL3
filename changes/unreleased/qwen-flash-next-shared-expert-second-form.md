## New features and improvements

- The Flash-Next shared expert's three dense projections of a prefill chunk (gate and up 2,560 -> 640, down 640 -> 2,560, 4 to 8 bits, 1,024 rows or more) run a second form of the large-row EXL3 linear. Each of its 8 warps owns 16 columns of a 64-row group's 128-column strip and runs every K chain itself, so the K quarters no longer meet through shared memory (128 barriers a block on gate/up), and the weights and the rotated input stream through a cp.async ring. Every output is bit for bit the first form's and the decode path's. On a steady 8,192-row chunk the 144 launches take 73.2 ms instead of 104.5 ms (0.51 ms a launch instead of 0.73); in isolation, rotation included, gate/up takes 0.83 ms instead of 1.01 ms and down 0.64-0.65 ms instead of 0.90-0.91 ms at 5 and 6 bits. `REXL3_BIG_SECOND_FORM=0` restores the first form.
