## New features and improvements

- Qwen3.8-Flash-Next's MTP draft chain stops when its next draft is not worth a step. The chain now runs in pieces (the catch-up pass and its first step as one graph, then one step a graph, with a host read of about 0.1 ms between them) and stops once the product of the head's probabilities of its drafts falls below 0.5 (`REXL3_MTP_EXIT`; 0 runs every step). The earlier policy ran as many steps as its last round had accepted drafts plus two, whether or not the head was still confident (`REXL3_MTP_ADAPT=1` restores that depth rule). Only how many drafts are made and verified changes, never which token is committed: serial replies, drafted == serial and concurrent == solo are unchanged, and the pieces make the drafts and head probabilities of the one-graph chain bit for bit. Measured on one GB10 (full benchmark run, replies exact, 9 pinned replies equal): 71.3 to 75.1 tok/s drafted decode (`tokens_per_round` 2.9194 to 3.0656), 90.3 to 92.2 tok/s aggregate on two streams and 108.7 to 107.8 on four (within the 1% noise of that sweep), cold 32K prefill unchanged (1,473 and 1,479 tok/s).
