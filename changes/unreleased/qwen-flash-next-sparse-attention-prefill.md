## New features and improvements

- Qwen3.8-Flash-Next prompt prefill spends less time in the sparse attention of its full-attention layers, with every output bit unchanged (the partial sums of every row and head are the same bits as before, and as the one-row decode kernel's; serial, drafted, prefill, needle and 2,048-row-chunk replies equal the pinned ones): a warp reduces the eight tokens and four heads it works on together, with the same additions in the same tree as the per-token warp sums, takes each score's max and exponential once instead of in every lane, skips the running-max rescale when the max did not move, and gathers each token's K/V row through offsets computed 32 tokens at a time. A 4,096-row chunk at 512 selected blocks takes 41.2 ms per layer before and 25.1 ms after (attention plus merge, one GB10); the attention kernel takes 489 ms of a steady 4,096-row prefill chunk before and about 298 ms after, and cold 32K prefill goes from 1,473 to 1,577 tok/s.
