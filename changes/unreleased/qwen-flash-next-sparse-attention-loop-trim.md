## New features and improvements

- Prompt prefill of Qwen3.8-Flash-Next spends less time in its sparse attention kernel, with every output bit unchanged (tested equal to the global-memory kernel at every position of a sequence's first 2,300 tokens and at 64 to 8,192 rows at random positions of four sequences, odd counts included). The kernel is limited by instruction issue and its floating-point operations cannot be fused or packed without changing a bit, so the work that is not arithmetic was cut: the addresses of the copies and reads are built once instead of in every group of eight tokens, a warp reads its three row offsets with one 16-byte read and issues six predicated asynchronous copies, the last short group of a row runs outside the loop, and the table of row offsets is refilled in three steps on different groups so that its two dependent global reads no longer stall the block. The kernel issues 3.6% fewer instructions (8.44 to 8.13 billion for a 4,096-row chunk); that chunk takes 22.75 ms instead of 24.07 ms a layer.
