## New features and improvements

- The decode and verify windows of four to seven rows now read the Flash-Next hyper-connection mixers (down, inject and up weights) from the lossless 13-bit packed copy as well, and windows of eight rows read the up weights from it; the packed kernels used to stop at three rows. The down and inject kernel no longer requests a warp's whole 16.9 KB of weights before it computes anything: it streams them in five stages, two ahead of the stage in use, and decodes a chunk's four rows before it loops over the window's rows, which keeps a warp's weights and x rows inside the register file (at four rows and more the old form spilled). Every sum is unchanged (the packed kernels are pinned to the fp16 ones bit for bit at one to eight rows). Measured on one GB10 in the real decode graph, median window span at 4 / 5 / 6 / 7 rows: 34.80 / 39.67 / 42.22 / 45.44 ms before and 33.80 / 39.00 / 41.60 / 44.81 ms after; a captured graph of 24 mixers, per call at 4 / 5 / 6 / 7 / 8 rows: 59.2 / 60.2 / 62.4 / 62.9 / 60.7 us from fp16 and 49.4 / 51.2 / 52.0 / 54.3 / 57.0 us packed. At one to three rows the new kernel takes 48.5-48.7 us a call, as the old one did.
