## New features and improvements

- Qwen3.8-Flash-Next's verify window overlaps its host work with the GPU. The window is launched as three graphs (the layers before the n-gram stage, the n-gram stage with the next three layers, the rest), so the host's launch of the large last graph (about 0.6 ms) runs while the GPU executes the middle one instead of in front of it, and the 36 conv-history commits that follow a window are one launch instead of 36. Every output bit is unchanged (serial replies, drafted == serial, `tokens_per_round` 2.9194 before and after). Measured on one GB10 (decode screen of six prompts, same node, back to back): 67.75 tok/s with the two-graph window, 68.44 with the three-graph one; the GPU's idle time inside a three-row drafted round fell from 865 to 280 us and the round from 37.70 to 37.21 ms.
